# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Single standalone HTML file (inline CSS and JS, no build step). Must open from disk, be shareable as one file, and host anywhere static.

## Users

Complete beginners to tech, often switching in from an unrelated field (e.g. health or sciences), who do not yet know which engineering roles exist or what to learn first. They arrive curious and somewhat overwhelmed, usually browsing on a laptop or phone in spare time.

## Product Purpose

A free, beginner-friendly map of tech engineering careers. Two jobs, in order:

1. **Explore:** discover which roles exist, what each is like day to day, and whether it fits you.
2. **Follow:** pick a role and see the step-by-step skills path to reach it, with a small project and free resources at each step.

Success: a beginner leaves knowing two or three roles that interest them and the next concrete thing to learn.

## Positioning

One connected map instead of separate roadmaps: every role traces back to three shared starting points (Programming basics, Math and physics, Electronics basics), so a beginner sees how skills overlap across software, AI, hardware, robotics, energy and health tech, and that early learning is never wasted.

## Operating Context

Self-directed study. Users open links to free external courses (CS50, Khan Academy, MIT OCW, etc.) and build small practice projects. No accounts, no backend.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- ~167 nodes: Skills, Tools, Projects, Fields and Roles (~68 roles in ~10 groups: Software and web, Cloud, AI and data, Games and design, Business, Health/medical, Electronics, Robotics, Energy, Research).
- Each node: plain-language note, one small project, free resource links. Each role has "what it is", day-to-day tasks, and "fits you if".
- Existing features: interactive graph with expand/collapse, search, type filters, roles list with group/prerequisite filters and sorting, role detail modal, "show path to role".
- Resources are free as of October 2026; not an official curriculum.

## Brand Commitments

- Neutral, impersonal voice. No references to any specific person or their background (remove phrasing like "her strong suit").
- Health-tech and dental-tech roles stay as ordinary fields among many, written neutrally.

## Evidence on Hand

All content lives in `Tech Engineering Pathways.html` (RAW node data, INFO role details, GROUPS). No testimonials, user counts or endorsements exist; do not fabricate any.

## Product Principles

1. Beginner first: plain language, no jargon without explanation, never assume prior tech knowledge.
2. Explore before commit: roles are the doorway; the skills graph is the follow-through.
3. Always show the next step: every view should answer "what do I learn next?".
4. Free and honest: only free resources, no hype, no promises about jobs or salaries.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard operable, screen-reader labelled, light and dark themes, reduced-motion respected, works at phone width.
